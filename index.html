A small hotel, run by the family that built it

Nilwella Bay opened in 2009 with four rooms and one cook. It has grown to fourteen rooms and a restaurant that seats sixty, and it is still the same family at the front desk in the morning.

Guests come for the quiet end of the bay. Fishing boats go out before dawn, the water is calm enough to swim from March to October, and the nearest main road is far enough away that you hear the sea instead of it.

All services in detail

Two kitchens, one terrace

The chef trained in Colombo and cooks Chinese to order — fried rice, noodles, devilled chicken, whatever came in from the boats that morning. The second half of the menu is Italian: hand-stretched pizza, pasta and lasagna from the wood oven.

Breakfast is served from 7am on the terrace, and includes hoppers, string hoppers, fruit and eggs cooked how you ask.
